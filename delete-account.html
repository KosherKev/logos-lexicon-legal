Delete your Logos Lexicon account

How to delete your account and cloud-synced data

Steps (in the Logos Lexicon app)

Logos Lexicon works without an account. An account exists only if you chose to turn on optional cloud sync. To delete it:

  1. Open the Logos Lexicon app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Cloud sync & account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

Deletion happens immediately. No email, support ticket or waiting period is needed.

Can't open the app?

Email kevinafenyo@icloud.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Logos Lexicon account deletion". We will delete your account and synced data and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account (your email address and sign-in details).
  • Everything synced to the cloud: bookmarks, notes and highlights, saved vocabulary, reading plans and progress, devotional progress, and your dream journal.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion, and there is no additional retention period.
  • Study data stored only on your device stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Deletion cannot be undone.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how Logos Lexicon handles data.
