Logos Lexicon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

The app works fully with no account. Your study data stays on your device unless you choose to turn on cloud sync. There is no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, saved vocabulary, dream journal, reading plans and progress, devotional completion, chosen theme, and any API keys you add are stored only on your device using standard on-device storage. Downloaded chapters are cached on-device for offline reading. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Optional cloud sync (account)

If — and only if — you create an account in Settings → Cloud sync, the app stores a copy of your study data (bookmarks, notes, vocabulary, dreams, reading plans and progress) with your email address in a private, per-user record, so it can appear on your other devices. This is done through Supabase. This data is used solely to provide the sync feature. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to track you. You can stop syncing by signing out, and you can request deletion of your account data at the contact below.

Optional AI companion

If you enable the AI companion, your typed question and the app's study context are sent directly from your device to the service you chose — your own deployed endpoint, or Groq / Google (Gemini) / Anthropic using your own key. Those requests are governed by that provider's privacy policy. No account or personal profile data is attached. If you never enable it, nothing is sent.

Bible text and lexica

Scripture text and original-language data are fetched from free, open, no-authentication sources (bible-api.com, getbible.net, and public GitHub repositories for MorphGNT and morphhb). Only the passage you request is sent to those services; no personal data is included.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Logos Lexicon contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone unless an adult chooses to create the optional sync account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
