Do I need an account to use the app?
No. Study, Read & Listen, Prayer, Devotional and Languages all work fully offline with no sign-in. The only account is the optional cloud sync in Settings, which lets your bookmarks, notes, vocabulary and dream journal follow you to another device.
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything you save is stored on your device by default. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on cloud sync yourself. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I read the Bible in the original languages?
Open Study from the home screen, choose Greek, Hebrew, Latin, or Septuagint, and tap any word for its lemma, parsing, and gloss. The Latin lexicon (Whitaker's WORDS) works fully offline.
How does the optional AI companion work?
You provide your own API key (Groq, Google Gemini, or Anthropic) or your own deployed endpoint in Settings. Your question is sent straight from your device to the provider you chose — we never see it.
How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email kevinafenyo@icloud.com with a description of what happened, the screen you were on, and your iOS version.
How do I delete my account or synced data?
Sign out in Settings → Cloud sync to stop syncing at any time, or email us to request full deletion of your account and synced data.
